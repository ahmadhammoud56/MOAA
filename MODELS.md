## Arena-forged council members

A forged member keeps the model and effort it was validated with in the arena. Both are written into its card, `~/.claude/agents/council-forged-<slug>.md`, and into `~/.claude/specialist-council/forged/registry.json`. `council.py resolve --role forged-<slug>` returns them, and `council.py check` verifies that card and registry agree. No forged members ship with MOAA.

## Agent Arena (`agent-arena-mode@moaa`)

- `agent-arena-judge` agent: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high (frontmatter of `agents/arena-judge.md`).
- Per-run assignments come from `scripts/arena.py`, keyed by task difficulty:

| Difficulty | Workers (tier/effort, share) | Judge | Critic | Synthesizer | Validator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| low | haiku/medium 50%, sonnet/low 50% | opus/medium | sonnet/medium | opus/high | opus/medium |
| medium | sonnet/medium 75%, haiku/medium 25% | opus/high | sonnet/high | opus/high | opus/high |
| high | sonnet/high 60%, opus/high 40% | fable/high | opus/high | fable/high | fable/high |
| critical | opus/high 60%, fable/high 20%, sonnet/high 20% | fable/xhigh | opus/xhigh | fable/xhigh | fable/xhigh |

Tier names map to the model IDs above (`arena.MODEL_IDS` must equal the council's `profile.json` models; a test enforces this).
